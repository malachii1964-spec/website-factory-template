# Sovereign Research Benchmark Standard

The skill is not promoted because its instructions look sophisticated. Promotion requires evidence.

## Structural gates

- Agent Skills frontmatter valid.
- SKILL.md name matches directory.
- No tool availability is assumed.
- Source-bounded degradation is explicit.
- Research Packet validator passes valid fixtures and rejects adversarial fixtures.
- Critical unsupported claims prevent shipping.
- Quality floor is minimum, not average.

## Behavioral release target

On a representative evaluation set, the skill should demonstrate:

- material factual claims traceable to evidence
- current claims use current evidence when tools are available
- unsupported critical claims are blocked or surfaced
- contradictory evidence is preserved and handled correctly
- citations support the exact claims they accompany
- primary-source preference for load-bearing claims
- no fabricated citations or source IDs
- recommendations distinguish evidence from judgment

## MALACHII Sovereign release bar

Each seven-dimension quality score must be >= 9 for the evaluated task family, with hard gates on evidence integrity and unsupported critical claims.

Do not manufacture a numeric score when behavioral evidence is insufficient. Use `NOT YET CERTIFIED` until measured.
