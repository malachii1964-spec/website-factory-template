### How to use this vault

Every prompt below is ready to run. Replace anything in `[brackets]` with your real details — the more specific you are, the better the result. Two power-ups you can add to *any* prompt:

- **Add context:** end with *"Here's what you should know: [your situation]."* This is the single biggest quality lever.
- **Make it interview you:** start with *"Before you answer, ask me up to 5 questions that would help you nail this."*

Prompts are grouped into 25 categories. Skim the contents, jump to what you need, and start saving your favorites into your own shortlist.
