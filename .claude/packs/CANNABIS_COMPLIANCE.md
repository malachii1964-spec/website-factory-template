# Pack — Cannabis Education, Cultivation, Wellness, and Commerce

## Scope

This pack adds conservative claim, source, jurisdiction, age, safety, and marketplace controls. It is not legal or medical approval.

## Required classification

- jurisdiction(s) and audience age;
- education, cultivation, product discovery, wellness, marketplace, dispensary, or clinical/research context;
- regulated transaction versus informational resource;
- personal health/grow data sensitivity;
- local licensing/advertising/age-gating/shipping constraints;
- evidence tier for scientific/cultivation content.

## Source states

- primary/authoritative research or regulator;
- qualified expert/extension guidance;
- reputable secondary synthesis;
- user/community experience labeled anecdotal;
- inference/unknown.

Do not turn cultivation anecdote, animal/in-vitro research, strain marketing, or a practitioner quote into a proven human health outcome.

## Visual and claim integrity

- cannabis imagery, medical aesthetics, lab coats, institution logos, data charts, and “science-backed” language create implied claims and require review;
- no unverified Mayo Clinic/Cleveland Clinic/Johns Hopkins/Harvard or similar affiliation;
- no fake analyzed-sample/customer/physician metrics;
- distinguish education from diagnosis/treatment and content from individualized medical advice;
- age/jurisdiction notices must match actual site behavior and cannot cure illegal/unsupported marketing.

## Data/product rules

Track source, date, jurisdiction, cultivar/product identity, method/limitations, confidence, and “what not to do.” For product discovery, separate factual label/COA data, retailer claims, user reports, and editorial inference.

## Release evidence

Jurisdiction/age decision, regulator/current-source research, claims ledger, asset/trademark permission, privacy data flow, qualified human review where required, safe disclaimers that do not contradict the net impression, and blocked-content list.
